{# Founding principles #}

Principles

Democratic

Founded by social platforms, for social platforms in Europe.

Inclusive

Representing all social platforms — big and small, new and established.

Open

Born to support innovation and growth in a spirit of openness and collaboration, celebrating diversity and European values.

{# What the organisation does #}

An organisation that…

  1. 01 — People

    Has a core staff led by a great, entrepreneurial leader.

  2. 02 — Gathering

    Continues the Rebuild spirit and format through an annual gathering that becomes an icon of the industry.

  3. 03 — Ecosystem

    Helps build an ecosystem where present and future social platform entrepreneurs and owners have the resources, knowledge and skills to realise their dreams and aspirations.

  4. 04 — Voice

    Creates strong visibility, a clear voice and a powerful presence for a positive, responsible and competitive social platform industry.

  5. 05 — Reinvention

    Becomes an example of the reinvention of industry associations for the 21st century — more democratic, more participatory, more dynamic.

  6. 06 — Funding

    Secures the funding needed to create and maintain a strong presence in Europe.

What if more than 100 — or 200 — social platforms founded their own organisation?
